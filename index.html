SUSTAINABILITY
THROUGH THE AGES

Explore Intel's journey through time, discovering how our commitment to innovation has shaped a more sustainable future for technology and our planet.

1968

Intel Founded

Robert Noyce and Gordon Moore establish Intel, laying the foundation for decades of semiconductor innovation.

Intel’s early research culture helped shape the modern technology industry.

1971

First Microprocessor

Intel introduces the 4004, the world’s first commercial microprocessor.

This breakthrough enabled smaller, more efficient computing systems.

1978

8086 Processor

The 8086 processor establishes the x86 architecture still used today.

Standardized architectures improved performance and energy efficiency.

1985

386 Processor

Intel launches the 386 processor with 32-bit architecture.

This advancement enabled multitasking and more powerful applications.

2006

Peak GHG Emissions

Intel reaches peak greenhouse gas emissions and begins major reductions.

Investments in clean manufacturing and renewable energy follow.

2020

RISE Strategy

Intel launches its RISE strategy to advance responsible innovation.

Focus areas include climate action, water, and waste reduction.

2022

Net-Zero by 2040

Intel commits to achieving net-zero greenhouse gas emissions by 2040.

This goal builds on years of environmental progress.

2023

99% Renewable Energy

Intel achieves 99% renewable electricity usage worldwide.

Clean energy sourcing significantly reduces Intel’s carbon footprint.

2024

Sustainability Summit

Intel hosts its first Sustainability Summit with global partners.

Collaboration accelerates sustainable semiconductor manufacturing.
